Create a Google review QR code, verify the destination, design a clear call to action, print it reliably, and request feedback ethically.

Get the correct Google review link

Start from the verified Google Business Profile you manage and copy its customer review link. Open that link in a private browser window and on a phone before turning it into a QR code. It should reach the correct business and lead naturally to the review experience without requiring customers to search again.

A QR code permanently represents the destination encoded inside it. If you print a direct review URL and later change the destination, the old code will not update by itself. Test carefully before ordering signs, cards, menus, or packaging.

Create and test the QR code

  1. Copy the verified review URL.
  2. Open QR Code Generator.
  3. Paste the URL exactly, with no extra spaces.
  4. Generate and download a high-contrast code.
  5. Scan it with at least two different phones before publishing.

Design and printing tips

Use a dark code on a light background and preserve the blank quiet zone around every edge. Do not stretch, crop, blur, or heavily decorate the code. Print a small sample at its intended size, then scan it from the distance a customer will actually use. Pair it with a plain call to action such as “Share your experience” so people know what happens after scanning.

Useful places for a review QR code

  • Receipts and thank-you cards after a completed service.
  • Checkout counters and waiting areas.
  • Table tents, menus, or takeaway packaging.
  • Post-service email or printed follow-up material.
  • Invoices where a customer has already received the product or service.

Ask for honest reviews ethically

Invite genuine feedback from customers without offering rewards for positive ratings or selectively asking only happy customers. Do not write the review for them. A neutral request builds more trustworthy feedback and avoids misleading future customers. Review platform policies can change, so check the current Google Business Profile guidance before launching a campaign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Read answers to the most common questions about this format and conversion process:

❓ Can I create a QR code that opens Google reviews?

Yes. Encode the verified review link from the Google Business Profile you manage.

❓ Do Google review QR codes expire?

A static QR code does not expire, but it stops working if the encoded destination URL no longer works.

❓ Can I reward customers for five-star reviews?

Do not incentivize positive-only reviews. Ask neutrally for honest feedback and follow current platform policies.

❓ How do I test a printed review QR code?

Print it at the intended size and scan it from a realistic distance with multiple phones.

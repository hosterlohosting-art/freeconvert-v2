QR code uses

QR codes can connect offline materials to online destinations. They are useful for menus, flyers, business cards, event pages, product packaging, WiFi access, and support pages.

How to generate a QR code online

  1. Open QR Code Generator.
  2. Enter the URL, text, or supported data.
  3. Generate the code.
  4. Download and test it with multiple phones.

Printing tips

Use high contrast, leave enough quiet space around the code, and avoid placing it on reflective or busy backgrounds. Test printed codes at the final size.

Tracking notes

If you need campaign tracking, use a clean tracking URL before generating the QR code. Keep the destination stable so printed materials do not become stale.

Security tips

Only link to trusted domains. For WiFi codes, prefer guest networks instead of private admin networks.

❓ Can I generate a QR code for a URL?

Yes. Paste a link into QR Code Generator and download the code.

❓ Can QR codes be printed?

Yes, but test the printed version before distributing it.

❓ Can I use QR codes for menus?

Yes. QR codes are commonly used for restaurant menus and event pages.

❓ Should I test QR codes before publishing?

Always test QR codes on multiple devices before printing or sharing.

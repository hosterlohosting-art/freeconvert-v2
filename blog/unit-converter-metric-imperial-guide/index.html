Why convert units?

Different countries and industries use different measurement systems. Unit conversion helps translate metric and imperial values for school, work, travel, shopping, recipes, fitness, and engineering notes.

Common unit types

Common conversions include meters to feet, kilograms to pounds, Celsius to Fahrenheit, kilometers to miles, and inches to centimeters.

How to convert units online

  1. Open Unit Converter.
  2. Choose the measurement category.
  3. Enter the source value.
  4. Review the converted result.

Travel and shopping

Unit conversion is especially useful for luggage weight, weather temperatures, clothing measurements, fuel economy, and distance estimates when traveling.

Accuracy tips

Round results appropriately for your task. Scientific or construction work may need more precision than everyday shopping or travel planning.

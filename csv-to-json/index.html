Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ How does the converter handle CSV headers?

The converter automatically uses the first row of your CSV as the JSON key properties. If your CSV lacks header columns, the tool can generate default generic keys (e.g. field1, field2).

❓ Is it safe to paste client databases or subscriber lists here?

Yes. Since all CSV processing, parsing, and JSON generation runs locally in the sandbox, no database records are sent over the internet.

❓ Does the CSV parser support custom delimiters like semicolons or tabs?

Yes, our engine automatically detects standard comma, semicolon, tab, and pipe delimiters instantly.

❓ Can I convert spreadsheets back to JSON arrays?

Yes. Column coordinates are converted to neat structured arrays, making them immediately ready for REST APIs and databases.

❓ What browser engines are required?

Works natively on Google Chrome, Safari, Microsoft Edge, and Mozilla Firefox on any PC or mobile device.

How to Merge PDF Files?

  1. Drop your PDF files into the dotted drag-and-drop zone, or click Choose PDF Files. You can add up to 10 PDFs, 50MB each.
  2. Check the page count of each file, then use the up and down arrows to put them in the order you want, or remove a file you do not need.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and wait while the pages are copied into one file in your browser.
  4. Check the total page count, then download your merged PDF.

Document Formats Glossary & Definitions

PDF (Portable Document Format): Adobe's fixed-layout document standard. It embeds fonts, vector graphics, and raster images identically, ensuring document formatting never shifts regardless of operating system or screen size.

DOCX (Word Document): Microsoft's fluid XML-based editing container. Perfect for drafting and copywriting, but susceptible to formatting shifts if opened on systems lacking the author's local fonts.

Merging PDFs: Combining two or more PDF files into one document, keeping every page in the order you choose. A good merge copies each page exactly, so text, links, and images stay as they were in the original files.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

You can merge up to 10 PDF files at once, 50MB each, with up to 500 pages in total. The merged file downloads as a single PDF.

Does this tool upload my files to any server?

No. All merging for Merge PDF happens locally on your computer inside the browser memory. Your files are completely safe and never touch our servers.

Will the text in my merged PDF still be selectable?

Yes. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text, links, and images stay just as they were in the original files. Nothing is re-rendered.

Can I change the order of the PDFs?

Yes. Use the up and down arrows next to each file to put them in the order you want before you click Merge PDFs. The merged file follows that order from first to last page.

Can I merge a password-protected PDF?

No. Password-protected (encrypted) PDFs cannot be merged here. Remove the password first, then add the file again. The other files you added are not affected.

Yes, works natively inside modern mobile browsers without app installations.

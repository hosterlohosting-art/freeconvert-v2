📄 Document Formats Glossary & Definitions

PDF (Portable Document Format): Adobe's fixed-layout document standard. It embeds fonts, vector graphics, and raster images identically, ensuring document formatting never shifts regardless of operating system or screen size.

DOCX (Word Document): Microsoft's fluid XML-based editing container. Perfect for drafting and copywriting, but susceptible to formatting shifts if opened on systems lacking the author's local fonts.

File Compression: The algorithmic optimization of document sizes, which identifies duplicate data arrays and compacts graphic payloads to fit strict email file constraints.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Can I combine multiple JPG files into a single PDF document?

Yes! freeconvert.cloud supports multi-page compiling. Simply select multiple JPG images and compile them into a beautifully ordered multi-page PDF.

❓ Does converting JPG to PDF reduce image resolution?

No. With the Best quality setting your images are placed at full resolution. A page size smaller than the image only scales it to fit the page — the original image data is kept. If you choose the Smaller file setting, photos are recompressed to JPEG quality 80%, which makes the PDF smaller but can soften fine detail.

❓ Are my private scanned documents safe from data leaks?

Absolutely. Because this converter operates client-side in your local browser memory, your scanned receipts or tax records are never uploaded to any server.

❓ Do I need Adobe Acrobat to perform this PDF compile?

No, our utility runs natively inside modern browsers (Safari, Chrome) without requiring plugins, apps, or setups.

❓ Does the converter work on iPhones and Android systems?

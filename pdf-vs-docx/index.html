PDF vs DOCX - Difference, Use Cases, and Conversion Tips

Understand when to use PDF or DOCX, how formatting differs, and which free converter to use for editable or shareable documents.

Quick answer

PDF is best for final sharing, printing, contracts, invoices, and layout preservation. DOCX is best for editing text, collaborating, and drafting content. Convert DOCX to PDF before sending a final version, and convert PDF to Word when you need an editable draft.

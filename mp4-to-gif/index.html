Video Converter Glossary & Standards

Animated GIF: A short looping animation made of still frames. GIFs play in almost every chat app, browser, and social feed without a video player.

Frame Rate (FPS): How many still pictures the GIF shows per second. 10 fps keeps the file small; 15 fps looks smoother but makes a bigger file.

Colour Palette: A GIF can only hold 256 colours per frame. The converter builds the best palette it can from your clip, so smooth gradients may look banded.

What is MP4 to GIF?

MP4 to GIF turns a short clip of a video file into an animated GIF. The sound is dropped — a GIF has no audio — and what you get is a silent loop that plays anywhere images play. It is built for users who need a quick, free, and private way to make short animations without installing desktop software. Use it for chat replies, memes, tutorials, and quick previews. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, so the workflow stays clear and predictable from start to finish. Keep clips short — a GIF stores every frame as a picture, so a 10-second clip at 15 frames per second is 150 pictures, and the file gets big fast.

MP4 to GIF format notes

A GIF holds at most 256 colours in each frame, and it has no sound at all. That is why short, simple clips look best — flat colours and clear movement survive the palette, while smooth gradients and dark scenes can look banded. The file size grows with clip length, frame rate, and width: doubling the width roughly quadruples the pixels. When the GIF comes out too big, shorten the clip first, then drop the frame rate, then the width. Keep a backup of the original video and watch the GIF loop once before sharing it.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The whole conversion runs in your browser with ffmpeg.wasm. Your video never leaves your device.

Which video files can I convert?

MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV and WebM. The result is always a GIF — the sound is dropped, and only the clip you picked is kept.

Why does the first conversion take so long?

The first conversion downloads the ffmpeg converter code (about 24 MB) into your browser. After that first download, later conversions start right away. Short clips convert fastest.

How long can my GIF be?

Up to 10 seconds of video. GIFs store every frame as a picture, so longer clips make very large files. Pick the best few seconds instead.

Why is my GIF so big?

A GIF stores every frame as a full picture, so size grows with length, frame rate, and width. Shorten the clip first, then drop to 10 fps, then to 320 pixels wide — in that order.

What Is a QR Code?

Learn what QR codes are used for, how they work, and how to create QR codes for links, menus, cards, and WiFi.

Quick definition

A QR code is a scannable 2D barcode that can open links, contact cards, menus, apps, or text.

Best use cases

Restaurant menus, business cards, product packaging, events, WiFi access, and print-to-web campaigns.

What to watch out for

Always test QR codes on multiple phones before printing.

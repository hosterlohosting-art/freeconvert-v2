Video Formats Glossary & Definitions

MP4: The most widely used video format. MP4 files hold video and sound together — a phone camera saves video as MP4, and almost every player and website accepts it.

MP3: The most widely supported sound format. It throws away some detail to make files small — a 3-minute song is about 3 MB at 128 kbps. It plays on almost every phone, computer, car stereo, and website.

GIF: A short, silent moving picture. GIFs show up to 256 colours per frame, so they are small and play in any browser — but they have no sound, and big ones get large fast.
